# WEFEH Framework: Water, Energy, Food, Ecosystem, One Health

This framework represents the core operational and legal structure of the **SIREP OASIS NEXUS TECH DZ** project, as part of the startup initiative under the **INAPI Patent N° 5893/2025**.

## 1. Water (الأمن المائي)
- Efficient irrigation systems for smart oases.
- Groundwater monitoring and desalination using renewable energy.
- Real-time management via **ThingSpeak IoT**.

## 2. Energy (الأمن الطاقوي)
- Integration of Solar PV and CSP systems.
- SCADA systems for energy grid optimization.
- Sustainable power for desert agricultural environments.

## 3. Food (الأمن الغذائي)
- Precision agriculture for date palm cultivation and oasis crops.
- Hydroponics and greenhouse automation.
- Traceability from soil to market.

## 4. Ecosystem (حماية النظام البيئي)
- Combating desertification.
- Enhancing biodiversity in traditional oases.
- Circular economy: waste-to-energy and organic composting.

## 5. One Health (الصحة الواحدة)
- Interconnection between environment, animal, and human health.
- Monitoring environmental pathogens and soil quality.
- Ensuring sustainable livelihoods for oasis communities.
